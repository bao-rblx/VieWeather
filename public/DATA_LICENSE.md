# Map data provenance

`asia.geojson` was derived from the Natural Earth country dataset distributed by https://github.com/datasets/geo-countries (retrieved 2026-11-01). Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.

Selected neighboring country geometries were simplified with Shapely at 0.012 degrees, preserving topology. Only the `name` property is retained. Geometry is for weather visualization and is not an authoritative statement of administrative boundaries or sovereignty.
